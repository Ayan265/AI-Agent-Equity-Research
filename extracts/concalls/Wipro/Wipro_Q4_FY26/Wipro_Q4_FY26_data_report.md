# Equity Research Report: Earnings Call Analysis

## Executive Summary
Wipro Limited reported a resilient performance for the quarter, characterized by stable operating margins and robust large deal bookings despite a challenging macroeconomic environment. The company continues to see strong traction in its Capco consulting business stabilization, alongside accelerating momentum in AI-led transformations through its Wipro ai360 ecosystem. Institutional investors should note the disciplined execution on margins, which expanded sequentially due to operational efficiencies and optimized utilization, offsetting wage hike pressures.

Regionally, Americas 1 and APMEA showed encouraging signs of recovery, while Americas 2 and Europe faced continued headwinds from soft discretionary spending, particularly in the BFSI and Telecom sectors. The pipeline remains healthy with a strong mix of cost-optimization and consolidation deals, highlighted by a landmark multi-year agreement with Olam Group to transform its digital infrastructure. Management remains focused on talent upskilling, generative AI integration, and rigorous cost management to navigate near-term uncertainties.

## Financial Performance
- IT Services Revenue at $2,625.9 million, representing a sequential decline of 1.0% QoQ in constant currency.
- IT Services Operating Margin expanded by 10 basis points QoQ to 16.4%.
- Net Income for the quarter stood at $341 million (INR 28.35 billion), representing a minor decline of 1.2% YoY.
- Earnings Per Share (EPS) was reported at INR 5.43 per share.
- Total bookings stood at $3.8 billion, with large deal TCV reaching $1.2 billion, up 12% YoY.

## Regional Performance
### Americas 1
Americas 1 grew by 1.2% QoQ in constant currency, driven by strong demand in Healthcare and Life Sciences, alongside a recovery in Technology and Media. Margins remained stable due to improved offshore delivery mix.

### Americas 2
Americas 2 declined by 2.1% QoQ in constant currency, heavily impacted by a slowdown in discretionary spending within the BFSI sector and delayed decision-making in Energy & Utilities.

### Europe
Europe revenue declined by 1.5% QoQ in constant currency. While the UK market showed resilience, continental Europe, particularly Germany, experienced soft demand as clients deferred non-critical transformation projects.

### APMEA
APMEA registered a growth of 0.8% QoQ in constant currency, led by strong traction in the Middle East and Southeast Asia, particularly in government and public sector digitization initiatives.

## Sector / Vertical Performance
### BFSI
BFSI remains the largest vertical but declined by 1.8% QoQ. Clients are prioritizing cost-optimization, vendor consolidation, and legacy modernization over discretionary digital transformation.

### Healthcare & Life Sciences
Healthcare grew by 2.4% QoQ, driven by strong demand for payer-provider integration, cloud migration, and regulatory compliance solutions.

### Consumer Goods & Retail
Consumer vertical grew by 0.5% QoQ, supported by supply chain modernization deals and digital commerce initiatives, though overall spending remains cautious.

### Technology & Communications
Technology sector declined by 3.0% QoQ due to ongoing restructuring and cost-cutting measures among major tech clients, leading to project ramp-downs.

## Large Deals & Pipeline
- **Olam Group** ($150 million over 5 years): A strategic multi-year partnership to transform Olam's digital infrastructure, migrate core workloads to the cloud, and deploy advanced AI-driven operations to enhance supply chain efficiency.
- **Leading Global Bank** ($80 million over 3 years): Selected to consolidate application maintenance and support services, leveraging automation to drive 25% cost savings for the client.

## Strategic Initiatives
- Expansion of Wipro ai360 ecosystem with a $1 billion investment commitment over three years to integrate AI into all platforms and solutions.
- Restructuring of the operating model into four Strategic Market Units (SMUs) to improve agility and client centricity.
- Accelerated training program, upskilling over 220,000 employees in generative AI fundamentals.
- Focus on high-margin consulting services through Capco and Rizing to drive downstream IT services pull-through.

## Headwinds & Risks
- Persistent macroeconomic uncertainty leading to delayed client decision-making and slower pipeline conversion.
- Reduction in discretionary spending, particularly in BFSI and Telecom verticals across North America and Europe.
- Wage inflation and talent retention costs in specialized technology areas like AI and cybersecurity.
- Geopolitical tensions in Europe and the Middle East impacting client confidence and investment timelines.

## Management Guidance
- IT Services revenue growth for the next quarter is expected to be in the range of -1.5% to +0.5% in constant currency terms.
- Operating margins are projected to remain resilient in the range of 16.0% to 16.5% despite ongoing macroeconomic headwinds.
- Capital allocation policy remains focused on returning 75-80% of net income to shareholders over a cumulative two-year period.
